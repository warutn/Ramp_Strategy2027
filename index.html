Slide 2 • Asset Synergies & Fuel Decarbonization

Asset Realignment & Green Fleet: Scaling Synergies & Slashing APU Burn

Capturing immediate operational efficiencies and bottom-line savings through GSE consolidation, Combo Units, and EV fleet transition.

Cross-Functional Integration

Engineering → Ramp and GSE Handover

Transfer GPU, ACU, and ASU assets + technicians to Ramp and GSE. Ramp assumes full preventive maintenance, daily checks, fueling, and FGS dispatch for APU-INOP flights.

ORGANIZATIONAL DIVIDEND:
Frees Engineering 100% to concentrate on aircraft airworthiness and hangar turnaround.
Jet A-1 Fuel Optimization

Combo Unit Scale-Up

Deploy ground Combo Units (Ground Power + Pre-Conditioned Air) at gates to substitute aircraft APU engine operation during turnarounds.

CFO BOTTOM-LINE IMPACT:
Substantial Jet A-1 fuel spend reduction & lower APU engine maintenance wear.
Fleet Modernization & ESG

Airside EV Fleet Conversion

Replace leased diesel utility pickup trucks with compact electric vehicles (EV buggies/golf carts) across short-haul airside workgroups.

COMMERCIAL & GREEN WIN:
Directly eliminates monthly lease invoices and curtails airside carbon emissions.
